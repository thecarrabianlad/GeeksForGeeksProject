What's inside

Six arenas, one campus

Every corner of NEXUS is built around a different way to compete, build, and watch. Move between them as you like — your badge gets you into all six.

Keynote Arena

A main-stage lineup of builders, founders, and researchers, closing with a live Q&A open to the floor.

Build Challenges

Twelve-hour hackathon tracks, judged live at midnight.

Live Showdown

Bracketed gaming and robotics matches, streamed on the main screen.

Night Circuit

The day closes with a full production concert on the main lawn — bands, DJs, and a closing set under the lights.

Creator Meetups

Small-group sessions with speakers and past winners, capped at twenty seats each.

Registration

Get your badge before the gates open

Entry is free for Bennett University students with a valid ID. Guests from other colleges can register below — badges are capped, so early sign-ups get priority seating at the Keynote Arena.

  • One badge covers every stage, all day
  • Confirmation and entry pass sent by email
  • Team registration available for Build Challenges

No payment required. You'll receive your entry pass by email.
